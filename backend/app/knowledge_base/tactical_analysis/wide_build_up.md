# Wide Build-Up And Overlapping Runs

Wide build-up systems create progression by stretching the opponent horizontally. Full-backs and wing-backs must provide width, overlap at the right moment, and maintain rest-defense balance when attacks break down.

Important indicators include crossing range, repeated high-speed runs, recovery positioning, and combinations with the nearest winger or midfielder. Players with strong stamina and tackling can support aggressive overlaps if concentration remains stable.
