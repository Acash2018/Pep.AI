# High Press And Quick Transitions

A high press requires coordinated jumping triggers, compact distances, and immediate counter-pressure after possession loss. Players who fit this model usually combine ball retention with fast decisions, pressing intensity, and direct forward passing.

Attacking midfielders in this system should screen pivots out of possession, receive behind the opponent midfield, and connect quickly with forwards after regains. Technical security matters because turnovers in central zones expose the pressing structure.
