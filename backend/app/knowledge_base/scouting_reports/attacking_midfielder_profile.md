# Attacking Midfielder Scouting Notes

High-value attacking midfielders combine chance creation, receiving between lines, and secure retention under pressure. In transition-heavy systems they should scan early, turn quickly, and release runners before the defensive block resets.

Recruitment risk rises when a player has low defensive work rate, limited aerial contribution, or depends on carrying without final-third decisions. The best fits for high pressing teams contribute counter-pressing actions immediately after turnovers.
