# Tiki-Taka

Tiki-taka is a possession-dominant system defined by short passing triangles, constant movement off the ball, and the use of possession itself as a defensive mechanism. The team starves opponents of the ball, manipulates them with patient circulation, and breaks lines once defensive shapes pull apart.

Ideal traits include elite first touch, scanning frequency, press resistance, tight-space passing, intelligent off-ball rotation, and confidence playing through pressure rather than around it. Defenders and midfielders must initiate build-up calmly while forwards drop, link, and combine in small spaces near the box.

Risk indicators include heavy first touch, slow scanning, players who default to long balls under pressure, weak counter-press reactions after turnover, and forwards who only function as orthodox target men.
