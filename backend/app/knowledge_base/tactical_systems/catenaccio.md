# Catenaccio

Catenaccio is a deeply defensive Italian system built on rigid man-marking, a sweeping libero behind the line, and disciplined counterattacking. The team concedes territory, denies central penetration, and looks to exploit opponents who commit numbers forward.

Ideal traits include reading of play, tackling timing, aerial dominance, defensive concentration, composure under sustained pressure, and economical forward passing from the libero. Forwards must be clinical with limited service, comfortable holding the ball under contact, and capable of driving long counterattacks into space.

Risk indicators include poor positional discipline, weakness in one-versus-one defending, lapses in concentration during long defensive spells, and forwards who require sustained possession or combination play to influence games.
