# Direct Play

Direct play prioritizes vertical progression, early forward passes, and quick attacks that bypass midfield congestion. The team accepts lower possession share in exchange for repeated entries into the final third and second-ball duels around the opposition box.

Ideal traits include long-range passing accuracy, aerial dominance, hold-up strength, second-ball awareness, channel-running pace, and willingness to chase lost causes. Midfielders must win duels around knockdowns, while wide players attack cutbacks and crosses from sustained early service.

Risk indicators include weak aerial ability, poor first touch under contact, slow transition recognition, limited running power in the final third, and forwards who need feet-to-feet possession build-up to be effective.
