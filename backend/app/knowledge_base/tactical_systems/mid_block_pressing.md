# Mid-Block Pressing

Mid-block pressing systems hold a compact shape around the halfway line, deny central progression, and trigger coordinated pressure when the opponent enters predictable zones. The block stays patient, prioritizes structure over aggression, and uses the touchline as an extra defender.

Ideal traits include pressing discipline, lateral mobility, positional intelligence, tactical patience, and the ability to read pressing triggers without breaking shape. Forwards must screen central passing lanes while midfielders shuttle to wide regains and protect the channels behind jumping defenders.

Risk indicators include players who chase out of shape, weak lateral movement, poor pressing timing, low concentration during passive phases, and forwards who only press individually rather than as part of a coordinated unit.
