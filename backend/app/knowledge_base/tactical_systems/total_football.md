# Total Football

Total Football is built on positional fluidity, with any outfield player capable of covering any role on the pitch. The team manipulates space through coordinated rotations, attacks with overloaded zones, and defends through aggressive offside lines and immediate counter-pressure.

Ideal traits include tactical intelligence, two-footedness, multi-positional comfort, strong scanning habits, ball-playing ability from every position, and high aerobic capacity. Players must read teammates' movements instantly, fill vacated zones without instruction, and contribute to both build-up and final-third creation.

Risk indicators include rigid positional habits, narrow technical profiles, players who rely on a single dominant foot, weak game intelligence, and limited stamina for sustained rotational movement.
