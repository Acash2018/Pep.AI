# Angeball

Angeball is built on aggressive possession, fast circulation, wide rotations, and relentless forward intent. The team looks to pin opponents deep, counter-press immediately after losing the ball, and attack the box with repeated runs.

Ideal player traits include bravery receiving under pressure, quick vertical passing, high running capacity, and comfort rotating positions. Wide players must attack space while full-backs or midfielders provide supporting angles. Central players must progress the ball quickly without losing compactness.

Risk indicators include slow decision-making, low defensive intensity, poor recovery habits, and players who need static positional structures to thrive.
