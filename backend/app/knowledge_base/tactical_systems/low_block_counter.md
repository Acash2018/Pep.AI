# Low Block Counter

Low block counter systems defend compactly, protect central areas, and attack quickly after regains. Players must tolerate long defensive phases, maintain concentration, and execute direct transitions into space.

Ideal traits include defensive discipline, recovery speed, aerial security, ball-carrying into open grass, and decisive forward passing. Attackers should run channels, hold up under contact, and convert lower-volume chances.

Risk indicators include weak concentration, poor duel strength, slow transition decisions, and players who need sustained possession to influence matches.
