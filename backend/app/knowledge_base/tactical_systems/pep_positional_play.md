# Pep Positional Play

Pep-style positional play prioritizes occupation of zones, controlled superiority, patient circulation, and high-quality chance creation through structured spacing. Players must understand when to hold width, when to invert, and how to create free-man advantages.

Ideal traits include elite ball retention, scanning, positional discipline, press resistance, disguised passing, and clean first touch. Midfielders and defenders must support rest defense while attacking players make coordinated movements between lines.

Risk indicators include erratic passing choices, poor spacing discipline, low patience in possession, and defensive transitions that expose the central corridor.
