# Back Three System

A back three system uses three central defenders supported by attacking wing-backs to provide width, with two or three central midfielders controlling the middle. The structure offers defensive solidity against front twos, build-up superiority through the wide centre-backs, and natural overloads in wide attacking zones.

Ideal traits for the wide centre-backs include progressive passing, ball-carrying into midfield, and recovery pace to cover the channels. Wing-backs must combine elite stamina with crossing quality and one-versus-one defending. The central defender of the three needs aerial dominance, organizational leadership, and clean distribution under pressure.

Risk indicators include wing-backs without the engine to repeat sprints both ways, centre-backs uncomfortable on the ball, weakness against teams that overload the half-spaces, and midfielders who cannot screen the gap between the back three and the front line.
