# Gegenpressing

Gegenpressing seeks to win the ball back immediately after losing it and attack before the opponent reorganizes. It depends on compact spacing, synchronized pressure, and high physical output from forwards, midfielders, and wide players.

Ideal traits include pressing intensity, acceleration, anticipation, counter-press reactions, vertical passing, and willingness to defend forward. Attackers and midfielders must trigger pressure together and convert regains into direct chances.

Risk indicators include low work rate, weak duel appetite, poor recovery speed, and technical insecurity that creates counterattacking exposure.
