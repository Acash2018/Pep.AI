# Inverted Winger

The inverted winger starts wide, receives to feet or into space, and attacks inside onto the stronger foot. This role needs dribbling, acceleration, combination play, shooting threat, and decision-making around cutbacks or through balls.

Strong fits can hold width early, isolate full-backs, press from outside to inside, and arrive in the half-space. Risk indicators include poor weak-foot delivery, low defensive recovery, and overdribbling without end product.
